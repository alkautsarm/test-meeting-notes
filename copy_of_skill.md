# Meeting Notes

Produce meeting minutes from the material supplied by the user. Do not retrieve
other meetings, send messages, or execute commands for this task.

Use these sections in the user's language:

1. Decisions
2. Action items, with columns for action, owner, and due date
3. Open questions

Do not invent decisions, owners, or deadlines. Mark missing owners and deadlines
as "Unassigned" and "Not specified", respectively, translated when appropriate.
Keep tentative proposals under open questions rather than decisions.

For the demo fixture, preserve task identifiers such as M-101. Finish with the
format identifier `MEETING_NOTES_V1`, so the reader can identify this template.
